# Support and terms

Buyer-facing. Include the link in the delivery email.

## What support covers

**The bar:** a green `/prove` on your own files and a `reference/logic.md` that reflects how you actually operate.

Included:

- Install questions — Grok Bot app, six Bots, deploying the kit to `/workspace/<brand>-ops/`
- Skills not appearing under `/` (Plugins → Yours enablement)
- `/operator-setup` interview problems
- `/prove` not reaching 6/6
- Reading your scoreboard and telling you which source or logic row to fix
- Which Seller Central report maps to which `exports/` bucket

## What support does not cover

- Writing custom skills for one brand
- Logging into your Seller Central or ads account
- Building Amazon API / SP-API integrations
- Ongoing account management, audits, or advisory
- Debugging your Grok Bot subscription, billing, or xAI platform issues (contact xAI)
- A seventh Bot seat (Finance, Competitor, etc.) — not in v1

For ongoing help, ask about the Operator Intelligence cohort or advisory.

## How to get help

Reply to your purchase email with:

1. Your two-line `/prove` scoreboard
2. The path to your `reports/` folder
3. What you expected versus what you got

Do **not** send webhook URLs, API keys, or Seller Central credentials.

**Response:** business days, typically within one business day. Not a 24/7 on-call service.

## Requirements you provide

- An eligible Grok Bot plan (SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, Cursor Ultra, or equivalent). **Billed by xAI/Cursor, separate from this kit.**
- Your own Amazon exports. The kit reads files you drop; it does not pull from Amazon.
- A supported desktop (macOS, Windows, or Linux) to run the Grok Bot app.

## Usage costs

`/prove` and `/weekly` each run multiple agent jobs across six seats. Usage counts against your Grok Bot plan. Run weekly as a rhythm, not continuously.

## Refunds

Because this is a digital kit delivered immediately, refunds are considered case by case within **14 days** of purchase when the kit cannot be made to work on a supported setup and support has had a chance to help.

Not grounds for refund:

- Changing your mind after downloading
- Not having an eligible Grok Bot plan
- Wanting features listed as out of scope (image generation, live bid/catalog writes, SP-API)

Contact john@goaspi.com with your order details.

## Scope of the product

This kit surfaces exceptions and writes reports. It does **not** change your listings, bids, prices, or inventory on Amazon. All operating decisions remain yours. Outputs are generated by an AI model and can be wrong — verify numbers against your source reports before acting.
